# Till Winter — Privacy

Till Winter collects no data.

- There are no accounts and no sign-in.
- There is no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.
- The game makes no network requests while you play.
- Your farm and your settings are saved only on your device, in the game's own storage. They are never sent anywhere.
- Deleting the game, or using Settings → Reset save, removes that data.

The game asks for no permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.
